# Rolling Dents Brand System

## Brand idea
Precision autobody work with visible proof. The identity should feel engineered, premium and direct rather than decorative.

## Primary lockup
Use the RD monogram with the ROLLING / DENTS wordmark. ROLLING is warm white; DENTS is signal orange. The location line sits below in steel grey.

## Compact mark
Use `public/brand/rolling-dents-mark.svg` for favicons, avatars and compact placements. Use `public/brand/rolling-dents-mark-mono.svg` where only one colour is possible.

## Colour tokens
- Carbon Black: `#090A0A`
- Charcoal: `#111212`
- Panel: `#1A1C1B`
- Signal Orange: `#F47721`
- Bright Orange: `#FF8733`
- Warm Cream: `#F4F1EB`
- Warm White: `#F7F7F4`
- Steel Grey: `#8F918E`

## Typography
Use a condensed, heavy sans-serif treatment for the wordmark and major display statements. Use a neutral system sans-serif for functional copy. Avoid script fonts, chrome effects and generic racing motifs.

## Usage
Keep clear space around the monogram equal to roughly one quarter of its width. Do not stretch, rotate or recolour individual logo elements. On dark backgrounds use the standard orange/white lockup. On light backgrounds use the compact mark or a dark wordmark treatment with signal orange retained as the accent.

## Voice
Short, confident and specific. Lead with the repair outcome, then the next action. Avoid proposal language, engineering jargon and exaggerated claims.
