# Rolling Dents Brand System — Production Identity

## Approved identity architecture
- Primary wordmark direction: Option D
- Compact icon / avatar direction: Option B
- Descriptor: AUTOBODY · LASER PARK
- Core positioning: premium, trustworthy, modern, automotive-focused

## Primary horizontal logo
Use `public/brand/rolling-dents-primary.svg` for website headers, footers, signage layouts, proposals, invoices and digital collateral.

The primary lockup combines the circular Option B RD icon with the cleaner Option D automotive wordmark. The roofline remains restrained and technical rather than illustrative.

## Compact icon
Use `public/brand/rolling-dents-mark.svg` for favicons, WhatsApp/social avatars, app icons and compact placements. It combines the circular badge, RD monogram, subtle wheel segment and precision spark from Option B.

Use `rolling-dents-mark-mono.svg` where one colour is required.

## Wordmark variants
- `rolling-dents-wordmark.svg`: dark-background horizontal wordmark
- `rolling-dents-wordmark-light.svg`: light-background horizontal wordmark
- `rolling-dents-wordmark-mono.svg`: single-colour wordmark

## Colour system

- Precision White: #F7F7F4
- Steel Grey: #8F918E

## Usage rules
1. Do not stretch, skew, rotate or redraw the logo.
2. Preserve clear space equal to at least half the height of the compact RD mark.
3. Use the primary logo on charcoal/black whenever possible.
4. Use the light-background wordmark on cream or white.
5. Do not add shadows, bevels, gradients or additional colours to the production logo.
6. Use the compact mark without the wordmark only where space is constrained or where the brand name is already visible.

## Brand voice
Direct, technically confident and customer-facing. Focus on the repair standard, clarity of process and the visible finish. Avoid inflated luxury language, internal engineering terminology and generic automotive clichés.
